## Key Data Points

### Market Statistics
- Fractional CTO market: **25%+ annual growth** through 2030
- Virtual CISO market: **$1.4B in 2024 → $3.8B by 2033** (12.2% CAGR)
- **72% of CEOs** plan to increase fractional executive usage
- Only **15.4% of seed startups** reach Series A
- Only **66% of Series A startups** reach Series B
- **445 ransomware attacks** on healthcare providers in 2025

### Cost Comparisons
- **Full-time CTO**: $486,874 average total compensation
- **Fractional CTO**: $120,000-$300,000 annually (60-80% savings)
- **Full-time CISO**: ~$870,000 over 24 months
- **Fractional CISO**: $192,000 over 24 months (78% savings)
- **Time to value**: 3-6 months (fractional) vs 9-12 months (full-time)

### ROI Metrics
- **Development velocity improvement**: 25-35%
- **Security incident reduction**: 50%+
- **Technical debt prevention**: $80,000-$180,000 first year
- **Time to productivity**: 2 weeks (fractional) vs 6 months (full-time)

### Healthcare-Specific Data
- **Average breach cost**: $9.77 million
- **Cost per compromised record**: $408
- **HIPAA penalties**: $137 to $2,067,813 per violation
- **FDA 510(k) timeline**: 112 days (fractional-led) vs 180+ days average

### Case Study ROI Examples
