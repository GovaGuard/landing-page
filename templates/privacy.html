Privacy Policy

Data Controller

GovaGuard GbR ("we", "us", "our") operates the website govaguard.com, which serves as a business-focused landing page.

Contact

What data do we collect?

We collect the following categories of personal data:

When visiting the website:

  • IP address
  • Date and time of access
  • Browser and operating system information
  • Requested pages

When contacting us via the website:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Organization name
  • Requested service
  • Message content

How do we collect your data?

Your data is collected in the following ways:

  • Automatically, when you visit the website (server access logs)
  • When you voluntarily submit information via the contact form

How will we use your data?

We use your data solely to:

  • Ensure the technical operation, security, and stability of the website
    (Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR – legitimate interest)
  • Process and respond to business inquiries
    (Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR – pre-contractual measures)

Your data is not used for advertising, tracking, or profiling purposes.

Third-party services

govaguard.com uses the following service providers:

Umami (web analytics). We use Umami Cloud (Umami Software, Inc.) for cookieless, privacy-friendly web analytics. Umami collects aggregated usage statistics (pages visited, referrer, browser type, country) without cookies, without cross-site tracking, and without building visitor profiles; IP addresses are anonymized and not stored. Analytics data is processed and stored within the European Union. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR – legitimate interest in understanding how our website is used.

Notion (contact management). Messages submitted via the contact form are stored in a customer-inquiry database operated with Notion Labs, Inc. (USA) as our processor. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR – pre-contractual measures.

The website is hosted by all-inkl.com, which processes technical access data as part of standard hosting operations.

For contact-form data processed by Notion in the United States, transfers are safeguarded under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or EU standard contractual clauses (Art. 46 GDPR). Beyond these services, govaguard.com does not use advertising platforms, cross-site tracking services, or embedded third-party content.

How do we store your data?

  • Website access data is stored in standard server logs generated by the hosting provider
  • Contact form submissions are stored in a customer-inquiry database (Notion, see “Third-party services”) and used solely for handling your inquiry

Data is deleted once it is no longer required for its original purpose or to comply with legal obligations.

Marketing

We do not send marketing or promotional communications and do not share personal data with marketing or advertising partners.

  • Access your personal data
  • Rectify inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Request data portability, where applicable

To exercise your rights, contact us at:
