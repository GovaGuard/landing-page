Quick answers

How accurate is this estimate?

As accurate as published ranges allow: your team size, infrastructure and locations narrow them, never replace them. A fixed quote follows the gap analysis.

Why does NIS2 show no audit fee?

NIS2 is law, not a certificate — no certification audit is mandated. The cost is implementing the Art. 21 measures, most of which a lived ISO 27001 ISMS covers structurally.

Do I have to leave my email?

No. The estimate runs entirely without contact data; booking a call afterwards is optional.
